04 / Good questions. Clear answers.

BEFORE WE
HIT THE ROAD.

Do you offer both transportation and dispatch?

Yes. This concept presents a 26 ft box truck owner-operator service for shippers and brokers, alongside separate dispatch support for independent carriers.

What types of carriers can request dispatch?

Box truck, hot shot, and flatbed carriers can submit an inquiry. Equipment specifications, operating authority, insurance, and lane preferences should be reviewed during onboarding.

What should I include in a shipment request?

Include pickup and delivery locations, dates, commodity, dimensions, total weight, and loading or unloading requirements. Equipment fit and availability must be confirmed before booking.

Are rates and service areas shown online?

Rates and availability depend on the route, shipment, timing, and equipment. This portfolio concept does not publish unverified pricing or service coverage.
